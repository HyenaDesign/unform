Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how Unform handles personal data when you visit this website or contact us. We keep data collection limited to what is needed to run the website, understand its use when you consent, and respond to enquiries.

Who is responsible for your data?

Unform is the data controller for the processing described here. You can contact us at sam.hyenadesign@gmail.com or write to Bergstraat 166, Kessel-Lo, Leuven, Belgium.

What data we process and why

When you contact us

If you email us, we process the address and any personal data you include in your message so we can respond and, where relevant, discuss or provide our services. We use this information only for handling that correspondence and any resulting work or legal obligations.

The project brief on this website is generated and downloaded in your browser. Its answers are not submitted to Unform by the website. If you later send the brief to us, we will handle it as correspondence.

Website usage analytics

If you accept analytics, this website loads Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google. GA4 may process information such as pages viewed, interactions, browser and device information, online identifiers and approximate location derived from technical information. We use this information to understand and improve the website. Analytics does not load and no analytics request is made before you consent.

Our GA4 configuration disables Google Signals and advertising personalisation signals. We do not use this website to serve advertising. For details about the storage used by analytics, see our Cookie policy.

Legal grounds

  • For analytics, we rely on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time using Cookie settings.
  • For messages you send us, we use the information to respond to your request and take steps you ask us to take before a possible agreement. We may also retain relevant correspondence where necessary for legitimate business purposes or legal obligations.

Who receives the data?

Email providers and service providers that operate the website or support its delivery may process technical information needed to provide their services. If you consent to analytics, Google receives analytics data through GA4. Google may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area; where applicable, international transfers are governed by the safeguards in Google's terms for the service.

See Google's privacy information for more about Google's processing.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up on the work discussed, and for longer only where needed for legal obligations or the establishment or defence of legal claims. GA4 event and user data is retained according to the retention settings of our Google Analytics property and Google's applicable service settings. The browser consent preference is stored for 183 days; see the Cookie policy.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal data, object to certain processing, or request data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email sam.hyenadesign@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or data processing changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Questions about privacy? Contact sam.hyenadesign@gmail.com.
