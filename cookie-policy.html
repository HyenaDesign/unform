Last updated: 2 October 2026

This page explains the cookies and browser storage used by Unform at www.unform.be. We use Google Analytics 4 only if you actively accept analytics in the cookie banner. Choosing “Reject analytics” keeps it disabled.

Your choice

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the website footer. You can also clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. If you clear the saved choice, the banner will appear again on your next visit.

Manage cookies in your browser

You can configure your browser to block or remove cookies. If you block local storage, this website may not be able to remember your choice, so the cookie banner may reappear. Blocking analytics cookies also prevents the associated measurement.
