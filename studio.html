INDEPENDENT STUDIO FLEMISH BRABANT DESIGN FOR GOOD CAUSES
01 / WHY UNFORM Independent by choice. Collaborative by nature.

Good causes deserve good design.

Unform is an independent design studio in Flemish Brabant, Belgium. We work with non-profits, associations and changemakers to make their purpose clearer and easier to act on.

Design is how people meet your mission: through a sharper story, an identity they recognize, a website they can use, and campaigns that invite them to take part. We bring those pieces together so your work is easier to see, understand and support.

WHO WE WORK WITH Non-profits, associations and changemakers
